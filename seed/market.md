# Market and alternatives

We have started checking how families can already manage a child’s money. This is an early desk review, not a full competitor study or proof that families want PadalaPocket. The detailed notes are in [work/market-reference.md](../work/market-reference.md); source checks are listed in the [evidence ledger](../work/evidence-ledger.md).

## What families can use

| Option | What it offers | Why a family might choose it | What we still need to understand | Source checked 2026-11-07 |
|---|---|---|---|
| GCash Jr. | A verified minor can use supported GCash services through an account linked to a parent or guardian. The guardian is responsible for monitoring the account. | A family already using GCash may prefer to keep the child’s wallet in that setup. | How families use it for allowance, and whether its current controls meet their needs. Our review does not establish that it lacks any particular feature. | [Terms](https://gcash.com/terms-and-conditions/gcash-jr), [setup guide](https://help.gcash.com/hc/en-us/articles/39417503859737-How-to-create-a-gcash-jr-account-for-your-child); EV-026 |
| BPI Jumpstart | A child savings account with allowance-transfer and savings-learning tools described by BPI. | A family focused on a bank savings account and savings activities may prefer this route. | Whether the branch-based account and its tools suit families managing allowance across countries. | [Jumpstart](https://www.bpi.com.ph/personal/bank/savings-accounts/jumpstart), [Jumpstart Savings](https://www.bpi.com.ph/personal/bank/savings-accounts/jumpstart-savings); EV-027 |
| Maya account for minors/students | Maya describes an upgrade path for eligible 7–17-year-olds, with parent/guardian consent and identity documents. | A family already using Maya may prefer its existing account setup, if the minor qualifies. | The page we reviewed covers eligibility, not a full allowance feature set. | [Maya support](https://support.maya.ph/s/article/Can-I-upgrade-my-account-even-though-I-am-a-minor-or-a-student); EV-028 |

## What this might mean for PadalaPocket

PadalaPocket is exploring three ideas: managing allowance across distance, setting agreed rules for Needs and Wants, and giving students some control over personal funds. These could be useful differences for some families, but we have not shown that families want them or that they work better than existing options.

Some trackers only record virtual balances, while PadalaPocket’s prototype is intended to demonstrate Testnet payments. That is a difference in the demo, not proof that a real payment service is ready: real peso/GCash settlement is deferred, and the demo’s fiat payout is simulated.

## Keep in mind

This review is based on product information, not hands-on testing or a complete list of alternatives. We have not established how common these options are among OFW families, compared all fees, or measured demand. Don’t call PadalaPocket “the first” or “better” based on this review alone.
