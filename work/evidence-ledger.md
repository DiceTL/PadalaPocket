### EV-029

**2026-11-14 — App-managed family wallet feasibility.** Read-only source/network investigation and isolated helper checks; no provisioning or architecture approval.

**Verified current layout**

- [Funding](../apps/api/src/funding.mjs), [purchase providers](../apps/api/src/purchase-providers.mjs) and [weekly ledger](../apps/api/src/weekly-ledger.mjs) use process-global owner, agent, merchant and student authorities, not a provisioned family signer registry. [New families remain financially fenced](../apps/api/src/identity.mjs). No secure per-family key store, provisioning/capacity lifecycle or receipt-to-family credit was found in the scoped application/migrations.
- [Bucket identifiers](../apps/api/src/weekly-plan.mjs) are deterministic contract-device IDs, **not Stellar accounts**. Needs, Wants and Savings use parent authority; My Money uses distinct student authority. The product's parent exception uses parent authority, not a mandatory extra exception account. One shared seller remains a shared signing/claim boundary.
- [Global funding admission](../supabase/migrations/20261107015615_l003_allowance_distribution.sql), [purchase admission](../supabase/migrations/20261107100607_l002_purchase_approvals.sql) and [weekly admission](../supabase/migrations/20261107114819_l004_weekly_lifecycle.sql) couple families. Removing global locks without replacing shared-signer and aggregate-seller coordination is unsafe. [Funding recovery](../apps/api/src/funding-recovery.mjs) explicitly fails with `RECOVERY_DISABLED_NO_DISPATCH_FENCE`.
- Scoped custody inspection found raw environment signing and historical private local journals, not application encryption/rotation/restore machinery. Hosting encryption, current local secret permissions, backup recoverability and deployed configuration were not verified. Signed envelopes are also submission capabilities and require protection.

**Executed checks and measurements**

Exit 0. No keys generated, signatures or submissions. This is not evidence of live family provisioning/isolation.

A candidate topology with owner, routine-agent source and student source per family therefore has **30 XLM in base reserves for ten families**, before principal, extra entries, contract storage/rent, fees and buffers. That topology is a proposal, not an approved account count or complete budget.
- Actual mapping helper: one family's cumulative ₱5,000 cap corresponds to **516.5289256 XLM**; ten independently rounded caps total **5165.2892560 XLM**. A lifetime cap is not a requirement to fund that entire amount upfront.
- Public Horizon reads of the EV-027 owner/agent and historical B-003 seller/student accounts found four funded accounts, each with zero subentries/sponsorship/native liabilities and one master signer. Observed owner balance was 9876.1220583 XLM. These separate snapshots do not prove available project float, current environment-key bindings, family entitlements or authorization to spend.

No current contract simulation/fee quote or device ownership readback was executed. Existing fee ceilings are admission limits, not measured normal provisioning costs.

**Proposed direction, not an implementation decision**

Bind each family/purpose/network to immutable public resources and versioned server-only key references. Prefer a managed secret store with separately restricted signing-runtime access over plaintext keys behind the database service role; encrypted database custody is an alternative only with authenticated encryption and independently controlled wrapping authority. Verify public-key matches and backup/restore in a fresh runtime before funding. Platform choice, access scope and restore procedure remain unresolved; encryption cannot protect keys from a compromised authorized signing runtime.

**Live-test prerequisites and safe sequence**

1. Establish approved secret storage, recovery operator, retained key references and a restore rehearsal; never rely on `/tmp` or flip `wallet_ready` as provisioning.
2. Select exact payer/resources and approve a measured reserve/principal/aggregate-fee budget. Prepare-only unsigned simulations can estimate current contract costs before signing.
3. Inventory unresolved database/local journals and coordinate every shared signer/writer. A database flag cannot revoke an already admitted dispatcher; never clear unknown work or replace an envelope merely because RPC returns `NOT_FOUND`.
4. Authorize a scoped one-family provisioning slice and durable operation manifest; verify exact ownership/policies/backing before enabling. Keep seller serialization and conservative existing fences until replacement coordination is proven.
5. On unknown outcomes, unexpected sequence/binding/pending funds or budget excess, stop and reconcile the saved identity. Recovery must return escrow while registry ownership still exists; the [pinned contract recovery implementation](https://github.com/noirx402/noir/blob/97c3808a204db96bf74558e53bf2271f9f5252ab/backend/contracts/payment_escrow/src/lib.rs#L318-L433) does not justify revoking the device first.
