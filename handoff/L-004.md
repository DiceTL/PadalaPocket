# L-004 — Combined weekly rollover

Authorized by DEC-088. Status belongs only in docs/implementation-plan.md.

## Implementation sequence

1. Add a server-only weekly clock/event journal and atomic admission shared with purchase/funding locks. Snapshot the unspent confirmed allowance, rules revision and target PHT schedule. Block advancement while purchases or funding remain unresolved.
2. Journal each exact signed Testnet envelope before dispatch. Withdraw Needs/Wants to reserved parent transit, fund Savings/My Money, and only then allocate the next allowance with the existing funding journal and the event UUID as its idempotency key. Execute one transfer stage per request; interrupted writers remain fenced.
3. Subtract swept principal from allowance availability and reservation checks. Display only confirmed destination credits; parent projections exclude My Money holdings. Keep real transaction/approval time separate from simulated scheduling time.
4. Connect parent schedule settings and Advance to next week (test), student recap, Savings and private My Money balances. Preserve loading, pending, blocked and skipped-allocation outcomes.
5. Check conservation, replay/concurrent admission, pending-operation blocking, role/privacy boundaries and staged interruption. Record automated and separately authorized live evidence here before acceptance.
