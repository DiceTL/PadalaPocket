Limitations: this is owner consent demonstrated by a trusted operator with local keys, not a built parent approval endpoint. The on-chain policy does not bind merchant/photo/bucket; the CLI enforces that signed request. A compromised exception key can redirect or split the available escrow before revocation; exact funding bounds total exposure but cannot guarantee purchase identity on-chain. Production custody, exception expiry/refund, distributed serialization, device lifecycle and wallet layout require team review and B-003/dependent task design. This experiment must not silently become the production topology.
