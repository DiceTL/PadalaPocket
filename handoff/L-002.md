# L-002 — Parent purchase decisions

## Scope

Priority 1 under DEC-074. Status lives only in `docs/implementation-plan.md`. Connect parent decisions to the existing Needs purchase pipeline; preserve its confirmed funding, idempotency, reservation, claim and no-blind-retry guarantees. Reuse the verified B-002 exception experiment only as feasibility evidence; it is not a product endpoint or journal.

## Required behavior

- Authenticated parent can list only that family's unresolved allowance requests and review the exact photo, amount, seller, AI result, category, policy snapshot and deadline.
- Requests needing review expire after ten real minutes. Parent approval/rejection is one-time, concurrency-safe and bound to immutable request fields; stale/expired decisions do not dispatch payment.
- Approval uses the isolated owner-authorized exception route from B-002 without raising or mutating routine policy. Persist the request, approval proof and signed operation before dispatch. Unknown outcomes reconcile by saved hash; no replacement envelope.
- UI presents waiting, expired, approved/rejected, payment-pending, confirmed and failure states distinctly. A decision is not ledger settlement.

## Delivery evidence

Test exact-field mutation, wrong-family/role access, expiry boundary, concurrent decisions, insufficient funds, exception isolation/revocation, duplicate requests and unknown outcomes. Verify the connected parent UI and an authorized demo journey only when safe. No live exception payment without a separately bounded exact request, funded isolated escrow and preserved recovery journal. Historical B-002 hashes do not satisfy product-flow acceptance.

## Limits

Do not add Savings requests here. My Money purchases remain excluded. Do not change the ten-minute product choice or routine cap. Do not claim full B-009 acceptance unless its original AI/category/insufficiency and review criteria are all met.
