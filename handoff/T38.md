## Behavior and acceptance

Three separable deliverables, all required:

1. **Route and authentication.** Add one authenticated `deposit_credit` path in the isolated coordinator (`apps/api/src/money-coordinator-handler.mjs`) that resolves the deposit's exact family/parent and calls `creditVeloDeposit`. It must accept no signing material, amount or destination from the caller, and must reject unauthenticated, cross-family and cross-parent requests.
2. **Trusted destination resolver.** Provide the coordinator-side resolver from family custody (`family-custody.mjs` / `wallet_custody`) for the parent account, pinned to Testnet. It must not be reachable from the public API.
3. **Project source and fees.** Provision a dedicated project deposit source with fee funding, bind it as the coordinator source, and record its public address. Family principal and reserves stay separate.

Done-check: `deposit-credit-route`, registered in `scripts/verify-backend.mjs`. It must prove a deployed coordinator call credits one verified receipt for the exact family exactly once, rejects wrong-family/unauthenticated callers, refuses an unverified or fenced receipt, and exposes no signing authority. The local check is green; the deployed call, the fee-funded send and independent review remain open.

The route is absent (`503`) until `PROJECT_DEPOSIT_SOURCE_SECRET` is configured, so no existing coordinator route changes behavior.
