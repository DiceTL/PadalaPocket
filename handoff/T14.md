## Decision and revised scope — DEC-127

The Testnet MVP uses a **designated operator payer**, not an app-managed payer and not a server-side Velo reporting adapter. The operator pays an admitted deposit's Velo hosted checkout from a separate funded Testnet wallet. The server verifies the paid intent and the exact Stellar payment before storing a receipt; no family balance is credited in T14.

- The parent needs no Stellar wallet. The checkout URL is payer-agnostic; the operator opens it and pays.
- The receiver remains the Velo project owner wallet. The operator payer must be distinct and is pinned by public key for the Horizon lookup.
- Velo exposes no merchant cancel/expire method; T16 handles cancellation as an app-level fence.
- No internal Velo reporter, app-managed payer, real fiat/GCash/Maya collection or partner automation is part of this task.
- Production partner/fiat/reporting work is deferred.
- [DEC-128](../docs/decision-ledger.md) records the owner’s direction to implement before T13 acceptance/merge; it does not claim T13 or T14 acceptance, merge, CI, deployment or a live send.

## Pre-implementation checks

- Create one checkout from an admitted deposit and pay it from the pinned operator wallet; confirm Velo reports `paid` and the operator is not the receiver.
- Confirm the paid intent exposes the identity, receiver, amount and asset needed, and that `payerAddress` (when present) is the pinned operator, not the receiver. If it is absent, use the pinned operator public key for the Horizon lookup.
- Confirm Horizon finds exactly one matching native payment and its transaction hash. Ambiguous or duplicate matches stay unverified.
- Confirm provider failure creates no replacement checkout and a browser redirect cannot mark a deposit paid.
