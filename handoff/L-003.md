# L-003 — Backed family-wallet flows

## Scope

Priority 2 under DEC-074, after parent purchase decisions. Status lives only in `docs/implementation-plan.md`. Build on the accepted DEC-049 physical topology and B-003 proof primitives; the operator scripts and their local journals are not product APIs or durable application accounting.

## Required behavior

- Connect parent-configured Needs/Wants settings to independently backed bucket funding and student purchases. Show only confirmed available amounts; enforce category/cap policy on allowance purchases and keep each bucket isolated.
- Move Needs leftovers to parent-owned Savings only after safe reconciliation; move Wants leftovers to student-owned My Money. Savings release requires an authenticated parent decision and one confirmed transfer into My Money.
- My Money spending is student-only, skips photo and parental rules, and uses its separate student authority. Parent APIs, activity, notifications and aggregates must not reveal its balance or purchases.
- Each movement is durable, idempotent and serialized with other operations using the relevant signer. Persist exact signed operations before network submission. Unknown operations retain reservations until reconciled; never retry with a replacement envelope.
- Fees and reserves remain outside spendable balances. Show illustrative PHP beside the actual Testnet XLM amount under the accepted fixed mapping; no peso payout is implied.

## Delivery evidence

Test conservation/rounding, empty and insufficient buckets, replay/concurrency, interrupted and unknown transfers, parent/student authorization, and My Money privacy across direct API, lists, photos, notices and totals. Demonstrate actual Testnet confirmation for every connected path before showing success. Verify parent and student screens with separate real accounts and inspect the browser bundle for server secrets.

## Limits

Weekly schedule, recurring allocation/reminder, week-end event advancement and in-app withdrawal are deferred to L-004/L-005. Do not call database-only balance edits backed transfers. The B-003 proofs establish feasibility only, not live product delivery.
