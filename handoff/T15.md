### 2026-11-23 — operator procedure for an expired credit envelope (F7)

- Scope: only a deposit whose T15 operation has a saved `credit` envelope that expired before a send claim. Do not prepare a replacement, sign a new transfer, or mark the deposit credited by hand; DEC-106 treats the preparation authority as retired.
- Procedure: read the operation and step state, confirm `phase='prepared'` with no send claim and no transaction hash, keep the deposit non-spendable, and record the deposit id, operation id, expiration and ledger evidence for the owner. The deposit stays uncredited and the family can start a new deposit because the T13 admission is settled (`status='paid'`).
- Any recovery that replaces the envelope requires a new T06 decision and review; this note does not authorize one.
