# Facts and statistics for the pitch

## A useful PSA fact

The PSA estimated **2.05 million OFWs** worked abroad from April to September 2025. During that same six-month period, total remittances were **₱240.73 billion**, including cash sent, cash brought home, and goods sent.

Possible Taglish wording:

> “Ayon sa PSA, tinatayang 2.05 million ang OFWs na nagtrabaho abroad mula April hanggang September 2025. Sa parehong six-month period, umabot sa ₱240.73 billion ang total remittances. Ipinapakita nito ang laki ng overseas work at remittances sa Pilipinas, pero hindi nito sinasabi kung ilang pamilya ang may ganitong situation o kung kailangan nila ng app tulad ng PadalaPocket.”

This is broad context only. It does not show how many OFWs have school-age children, how much allowance students receive, or whether families want PadalaPocket. The ₱240.73 billion covers six months and includes cash sent, cash brought home, and goods—not just cash transfers to children.

**Slide source note:** Philippine Statistics Authority, 2025 Survey on Overseas Filipinos; April–September 2025 reference period. See the [PSA release](https://psa.gov.ph/statistics/survey/labor-and-employment/survey-overseas-filipinos/node/1684084311) and [detailed notes](../work/facts-and-statistics-reference.md). Evidence: [EV-030](../work/evidence-ledger.md).

## Keep in speaker notes unless needed

The BSP reports that 86% of Filipino adults said they have a personal budget. That result is about adults, not the 13–14-year-olds included in PadalaPocket's target audience, and it is not evidence that the app would improve budgeting. See the [BSP report](https://www.bsp.gov.ph/Inclusive%20Finance/Financial%20Inclusion%20Reports%20and%20Publications/2025/2025CFISreport.pdf) and [EV-031](../work/evidence-ledger.md).

The detailed definitions, survey limits, and earlier 2024 figures are in the [research reference](../work/facts-and-statistics-reference.md).
