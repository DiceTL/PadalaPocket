# PadalaPocket dark-theme redesign prompt

Use the attached FE-003 desktop reference, FE-004 mobile reference, Piko mascot image, and dark dashboard style reference as visual inputs. First redesign Piko for practical website use; then incorporate that same redesigned mascot directly into both UI concept images. These are exploratory visual proposals, not approved product requirements or production-ready assets.

## Context

PadalaPocket is a family allowance application for Filipino parents and children. The desktop reference is the parent dashboard; the mobile reference is a student purchase-flow screen. They are different role-specific screens, not the same page resized. Preserve each reference's purpose, information hierarchy, recognizable controls, and state; reimagine the visual style without inventing product features or changing what an action does.

## Deliverables

1. **First: Piko mascot concept sheet.** Design Piko for a real website first, prioritizing a simple, reproducible 2D vector/SVG construction that can be animated through code. Show a neutral pose and a gentle guiding pose on dark surfaces, plus a labeled breakdown of independently movable head, eyes/eyelids, beak, body, wings and feet. Include a small-size view and clear overlapping joints or pivot points. Treat the sheet as a visual design reference, not a rigged asset.
2. **Then: Desktop — FE-003.** Create a full parent dashboard at a 1280 px-wide desktop viewport. Retain the sidebar, balance overview, parent review area and allowance plan shown in the reference. Visibly integrate the redesigned Piko into the interface itself in a compact, helpful contextual area.
3. **Then: Mobile — FE-004.** Create a full student screen at a 390 px-wide mobile viewport. Follow the provided FE-004 reference for its specific purchase-flow content, controls and state. Reflow for a realistic phone screen rather than shrinking the desktop dashboard. Visibly integrate the same redesigned Piko into the mobile interface, scaled and positioned for the available space.

Export each as its own image; do not combine the desktop and mobile screens into a single presentation board. Show the actual interface front-on, not inside a decorative device mockup. Keep all text legible and use realistic spacing.

## Art direction

- Use the attached dark dashboard as a reference for the overall SaaS feel: compact sidebar navigation, layered dark panels, structured cards, restrained accent color and a professional information hierarchy. Do not copy its unrelated content, financial metrics, charts or controls. Ignore its glass/translucency effect; solid, readable surfaces are preferred.
- A polished, dark-themed SaaS dashboard: deep charcoal or navy surfaces, restrained cool accents, distinct surface levels, clean sans-serif typography, and clear hierarchy. Aim for warm, approachable and trustworthy rather than childish, gamified or corporate-cold.
- Make it welcoming to Filipino parents and children without relying on stereotypes or generic cultural decorations. Use the peso symbol where it appears in the reference.
- Use readable contrast, clear focus and selected states, generous touch targets, and status labels in addition to color. Avoid excessive glow, visual noise, tiny text and unnecessary charts.
- Keep Needs, Wants, Savings and My Money terminology where present. Preserve simulation and sample-data labels where applicable; never make a pending transaction look paid. Use only clearly labeled sample data; do not suggest real balances or real transactions.
- Piko must appear as part of both redesigned interfaces—not only on the separate mascot sheet. Place the mascot sparingly as a helpful guide, such as a compact contextual hint, without adding a new feature or obscuring balances, status, navigation, or primary actions. Keep the mascot's design consistent across the sheet and both screens.

## Piko redesign and animation constraints

Use the supplied Piko the Pocket Eagle image for identity: a friendly eagle with an expressive face, recognizable crest, wings and pocket motif. Adapt its palette to stand out against dark surfaces while fitting the interface; explore lighter or more saturated body colors and clear outlines rather than simply making every part dark. Maintain a calm, trustworthy expression and strong silhouette at small sizes.

Design for a future code-driven animation implementation, preferably simple 2D vector/SVG parts; a similarly simple 3D interpretation is acceptable if it remains practical. Use clean geometric shapes, limited colors and shading, minimal feather detail, and clearly separated overlapping parts with plausible pivot points. Neutral pose should support subtle blinking, head tilts and a wing gesture. Avoid intricate textures, tiny details, complex deformations and photorealism. Respect reduced-motion preferences in any eventual implementation. The concept image itself does not prove the mascot is rigged or animatable.

## Constraints

Do not add new workflows, controls, balances, success claims or product promises not shown in the references. Keep the original screenshots recognizable as the basis for each redesign. If a detail is unclear, preserve the reference rather than inventing functionality. Treat all visual changes as proposals for the human design owner's review.
